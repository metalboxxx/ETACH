About these tests: Vietnamese school English tests (grades 6–12, e.g. "Kiểm tra giữa kỳ",
"Kiểm tra cuối học kỳ"). The header and some instructions are in Vietnamese; keep Vietnamese text
exactly, with its accents.
- Sections are numbered with Roman numerals (I, II, III …) or "Part"/"PART"; questions are
  numbered 1, 2, 3 … across the whole test. Points are often printed next to a heading, e.g.
  "(2.0 điểm)" or "(2.5 pts)".
- Typical sections: listening; pronunciation (underlined sounds) and stress; multiple choice
  grammar and vocabulary; communication; signs and notices; reading (true/false, multiple choice,
  cloze); word form (cue word in capitals); verb form; sentence rewriting and combining; writing.
- "Hình" or "ảnh" marks a picture (e.g. "Hình 1"); an answer sheet may be printed as an answer
  box or table ("Answer:", "Đáp án", "Trả lời").
